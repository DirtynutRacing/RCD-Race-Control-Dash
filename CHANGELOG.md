# Changelog

## v1.0

First release.

- Three pages — **Race**, **Inputs** and **Tyres & Fuel** — flipped by tapping the screen (SimHub's simplified touch mode) or with a mapped button / knob
- **Race:** gear, speed, RPM number and RPM bar, pit limiter, position, lap / total, current / best / last / predicted lap, delta with delta bar, optimal lap, gaps ahead and behind, fuel laps, brake bias, ABS and TC level, sector panels (incl. iRacing multi-sector), session time or laps left
- **Inputs:** brake / throttle bars, 5-second input trace, ABS / TC lights, peak brake, time to peak brake, wheel lock, FFB and clipping, brake bias
- **Tyres & Fuel:** tyre temps (inside / middle / outside), pressures, tyre left %, brake temps, full fuel strategy (left, per lap, laps, last lap, to finish, add at stop), water / oil temp, iRacing incidents
- RPM bar follows the car's own shift lights in iRacing and the game's max RPM in other sims
- On every page: full-screen spotter (CAR LEFT / CAR RIGHT / 3 WIDE), flag frame, low fuel warning, pit limiter
